# RadialActions privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

RadialActions does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting, and the app itself makes no network connections.

**What stays on your PC**

- Your settings, pinned and recent folders, custom actions, which actions you use most and the list needed to undo the last action. These are plain files in the app's data folder (`%APPDATA%\RadialActions`, or the package's private copy of it for the Store version).
- Your files. Conversions run locally. Text recognition for scanned PDFs uses the engine built into Windows.

**When other programs are involved**

- Some conversions use programs already on your PC (Microsoft Edge, Microsoft Office or LibreOffice). Those run locally under their own settings and policies.
- The settings windows use the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime to draw bundled pages. They load nothing from the internet.
- Actions you add yourself start the program you choose, which may do whatever that program does.
- The links on the About page open in your browser.

**Removing your data**

Uninstalling the Store version removes its data. For the installer version, delete `%APPDATA%\RadialActions`.
